About PawMart

A small team of pet lovers, building something for pets we'd trust with our own.

Our Story

PawMart started as a college project among four friends who each grew up with a dog, a cat, or both. We noticed how many pet owners were juggling five different shops just to find decent food, a comfy bed, and the right toy — so we set out to bring it all under one roof.

We're not a big retailer. We're students who wanted to build something useful, with a simple, honest shopping experience and no unnecessary clutter.
